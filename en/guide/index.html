Radio equipment study guide

Connect circuit behaviour with signal transmission, antennas and systems. Distinguish on-screen learning from actual equipment operation and official assessment.

1. Check the exact qualification and current criteria

Select 무선설비기사 on KCA’s qualification website and review current criteria, eligibility, required materials and schedules. Do not confuse it with another level or communication qualification.

2. Connect signals with devices

Consider how each device affects a signal and how antennas connect with feeders. Check the units and conditions stated in a question, then use the explanation to review the reasoning.

3. Prepare for equipment practice and safety separately

The official practical assessment is hands-on radio equipment work. On-screen questions do not verify assembly, measurement skills or safety procedures. Prepare equipment practice and current required materials separately under official guidance.
