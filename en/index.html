Before you begin

Is this an official KCA app?

No. RushLabs provides an independent study aid. It does not register candidates, award qualifications or make official pass decisions.

What does Pro mean?

A separate paid app with included learning content and no additional subscription. It is distinct from the original app; an original-app subscription does not automatically purchase Pro.

What language are the questions in?

The included questions and shown app interface are Korean. English website guidance does not mean translated app questions or an identical official test format.

Does it provide practical assessment or a public download?

It does not provide official hands-on assessment or equipment-practice certification. Download links are added only after a verified public Pro release. This site shows no prices.
